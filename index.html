Who is The Pathfinder Baptist Missions?

Under the authority of Grace & Glory Baptist Church, Abuja, Pathfinder Baptist Missions trains, sends, and supports pastor-missionaries planting churches across Nigeria.

Since 2020, we have partnered with churches at home and abroad to advance the Gospel through Nigerian missionaries.

Meet our missionaries
Meet Ptr. Adewale Adesina

A visionary leader, missionary, and ministry teacher.

Adewale Adesina was saved and baptized at the age of twelve. He felt called to preach at a young age and served for eight years at Victory Independent Baptist Church in Bodija, Ibadan, Nigeria.

He currently serves as pastor of Grace and Glory Baptist Church in Jahi, Abuja, Africa S.M.I.T.E. Director, and Founder of Pathfinder Baptist Missions. He is also an editor and teacher at Providence Baptist College and Seminary, Abuja.
