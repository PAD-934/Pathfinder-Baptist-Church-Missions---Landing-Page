About Our Mission

Sending the Gospel to
Unreached Nigeria

The Pathfinder Baptist Missions is a missionary-sending arm of Grace & Glory Baptist Church, Abuja. We identify, train, send, and financially support pastor-missionaries who plant Baptist churches in spiritually needy communities across Nigeria.

Each missionary family we support has left the comfort of home to preach the Gospel where it has rarely — or never — been heard. They trust God and depend on the generosity of the global church to sustain them.
